01 — About Me

In a Snapshot

I am an editor with two decades of experience delivering publishing-grade content across IT, manufacturing, and BFSI.

I have spent much of my career translating technical information for people who did not have the context to read it. I have worked in fast-paced marketing communication environments and edited scientific publications for environmental conservation organizations. I have also led editorial governance and mentored teams of content writers and editors, while establishing quality frameworks that cut rework.

My editorial instinct always favors clarity and directness over flamboyance. A defining strength of my practice is my command of referencing style guides and English variants. I am known for my ability to rewrite complex technical content into clear, simple language for any audience, global or local.

I live in Chennai and am pursuing an MA in Women’s and Gender Studies.
